All processing stays in your browser. No dependencies, pretrained weights, internet calls, or backpropagation. Webcam permission may require localhost / HTTPS rather than opening file://.

Start on the bouncing pulse. Train for two full cycles (168 frames), then switch to Shape only and watch the collision field. Toggle memory writes off to test a frozen model.

The exact spatial identity is x = core + middle + fast. The prediction is a weighted average of transitions recalled by a global core head plus two spatially shared 5×5 patch heads. The collision image sums component-wise recalled transition dispersion; it is an uncertainty diagnostic, not a calibrated causal probability. Free-run may collapse or drift.
